Process

How I work.

01
Discover

AI audit, stakeholder interviews, capability mapping. We start by understanding what your models can do — and where they break.

02
Define

Problem framing, UX principles for AI, success metrics. Crisp definitions become the contract for everything we ship.

03
Design

Rapid prototyping, interaction patterns, component architecture. Tested in front of real users — not stakeholders.

04
Deploy

Handoff, QA, design system docs, post-launch iteration. The first launch is a hypothesis — we keep refining together.
